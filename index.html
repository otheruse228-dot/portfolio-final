04 · RAG KNOWLEDGE BASE

Company Knowledge AI

An AI that answers from your own company documents, not from guesswork.

THE PROBLEM
General chatbots make things up when they do not know your business.
WHAT I BUILT
A knowledge base in Supabase with pgvector. Documents are split into small chunks and indexed; the AI first finds the most relevant passages, then answers using only them. Replies come back as structured JSON, so they plug straight into workflows.
THE RESULT
More accurate answers grounded in real company data, and fewer made-up replies.
Supabase pgvector Embeddings Semantic search Claude / Gemini JSON schema output
